Who we are

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you enter: your name, mobile number, email address (optional), city, the service you are interested in, your message, and any optional service details you choose to enter (such as property type, budget or roof availability). How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and for our business records, and as required by law. After that we delete or anonymise them.
